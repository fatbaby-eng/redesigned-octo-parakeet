About

I have always been someone who likes to make things.

When I was a kid, I took apart my Stomper trucks and built little creations out of the motors. That curiosity never really left. It just grew up and got a job. Then it grew up some more and started its own shop.

For thirty years I led graphic design at First National Bank of Omaha. Six business lines. Built the department from scratch. Hired the designers. Shipped the brand systems. Led the team through the 2020 rebrand to FNBO. It was good work. Hard work. The kind that teaches you how to make things inside an institution that moves slow on purpose.

Now I work for myself under Fatbaby Design. Same craft, fewer meetings.

I also practice with Flatwater Collective, an Omaha mindfulness community. I am a sangha member. I show up, I sit, I sometimes guide. Their teachers bring mindfulness into Douglas County and Pottawattamie County jails. I am on the build-and-support side, making digital tools to extend that work. I also started Men of Flatwater with Matt Evans — a monthly Saturday-morning circle for men who want to show up and talk.

After hours, I make a mix of things. Small design experiments. AI tools. Browser games. Woodworking projects. Garden beds. Photography with a Kodak Charmera. Nothing here is perfect. Most of it is in progress. That is the fun part.

This site is for the people who know me. Family, friends, anyone who wanders in. If you are here to hire, the work is in the Studio and the Bench. If you are just looking around, take your time. If you are looking for proof that someone can spend three decades inside corporate design and still stay curious, yeah. You found it.

The Studio

Thirty years of making things that last

1996 — 2005
Building the foundation

Started at First National Bank of Omaha as a designer. Back then the "graphics department" was a room and a printer. I built the systems, hired the people, and shaped the infrastructure that turned it into a functioning creative operation. Learned how to make things inside a bank. That is a specific skill.

2005 — 2020
Scaling the brand

Built the operational infrastructure that holds six business lines together: templates, guidelines, approval workflows, the machinery that lets a small team serve a large institution. Led my graphic designers through the rebrand from First National Bank of Omaha to FNBO in 2020. Taught design at Metropolitan Community College on the side. Different classroom, same lesson: show up, pay attention, share what you know.

2020 — 2026
Leading the team

Senior Manager of Graphic Design. Hired designers, ran the team, fought for craft inside an organization built to move carefully. Thirty years in, I closed that chapter and walked into the next one.

2026 — Present
Independent practice

Fatbaby Design. Consulting, building, teaching. AI tools, training walkthroughs, browser games, a private AI for my family. The soldering iron is back out. The camera is too. Same hands, more room to swing them.
